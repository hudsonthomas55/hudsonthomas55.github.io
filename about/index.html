A little about myself

I’m Thomas, based in Richmond, Kentucky. I like learning for its own sake — whether that’s a new book, a chess opening, a homebrew recipe, or a small coding project.

Outside of screens you’ll often find me on a trail, on the water in a kayak, crocheting, or quietly working through a stack of books. I brew beer for fun, follow chess closely, and keep a soft spot for Hogwarts houses — Ravenclaw, of course.

This site is intentionally small: a place to introduce myself and share occasional notes, without turning into a résumé dump.
